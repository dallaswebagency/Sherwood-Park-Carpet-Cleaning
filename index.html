3 things you can do to keep your carpets cleaner.

1

Vacuum weekly

Dirt that sits on carpet works deeper into the fibres, gets harder to remove and can let bacteria grow.

2

Treat stains promptly

Dab a stain, don't scrub it. Rubbing only spreads the stain further.

3

Book regular deep cleans

It helps your carpet keep its look for longer and can head off costly emergency call-outs or replacement.

How long Norovirus can survive on carpet fibres

Worth knowing

Does bacteria actually hide out in your carpet?

It can. Carpets can collect things like these:

Norovirus/Salmonella/Campylobacter

Norovirus, also called Norwalk virus, is behind stomach flu and other digestive problems. It can live on carpet fibres for four to six weeks and can even become airborne when people walk across the carpet.

Regular vacuuming and deep cleaning help remove these organisms and keep them from settling in.
